Broadcast is off. Sounds only play on your speakers.
How others hear it: a web page can't talk into another app's microphone directly, so Neon Pulse sends its sound to a virtual audio cable that Discord, Zoom, Teams, OBS or your game uses as its mic.
  1. Install a virtual cable: VB-CABLE (Windows) or BlackHole (Mac).
  2. Click Detect Devices and pick the cable as the Broadcast Output (on Windows it shows as "CABLE Input").
  3. In your call app, set the microphone to the other end of the cable (on Windows, "CABLE Output").
  4. Turn on Mix in my microphone so people still hear your voice along with the sounds.
  5. Click Pop Out Mini Board to keep the pads floating above your game or call. Works in Chrome / Edge on desktop.
